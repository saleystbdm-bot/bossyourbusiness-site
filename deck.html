05 What an operator's note is

It runs to 8 pages, and it is written from the book for a man running a factory.

  • 01Pages 1 to 4. What the book actually saysThe key points in plain language, in the order you would use them, with the parts that do not survive contact with your floor left out. And I say plainly what the book is, whether it is a school textbook, a memoir, or a consultant selling his own method.
  • 02Page 5. A diagnosis you score yourselfYou answer 10 questions yes or no and you get a score out of 10. It takes about 4 minutes, and it tells you where you actually stand rather than where you hope you do.
  • 03Page 6. A sheet you fill from your own numbersThe sheet works on your own figures rather than on the book's examples, and your figures are what make it real.
  • 04Page 7. What to do in the next 7 daysOne move, small enough to actually happen, named clearly enough that on Monday morning you know exactly what to do first.
