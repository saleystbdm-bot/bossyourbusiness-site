02 Why this exists

The books are written in English, and your evenings are already taken.

Every owner has been told to read more, and most of them have bought the books. The books are still lying on the shelf, and the reason is not a lack of discipline. A business book runs 300 pages, it is written in English, and its examples come from American companies that have a sales department, a marketing head and a board that meets every quarter.

Your business runs on a different set of facts. One large buyer squeezes the price every quarter, you carry 40 tasks yourself, and there is nobody second in command to hand them to. The next generation is waiting for its turn and is not certain it wants it. The ideas in these books do answer those problems, but only after somebody carries them across into your language and your kind of business.

I read more than 100 books a year, and for 9 years the best of it reached owners one at a time, sitting in front of me in Chennai. This carries it further, in the language most of them think in even on the days they work in English.

05 The 6 questions

Every book on the shelf answers 1 of 6 questions.

The order is the order a business gets diagnosed in rather than the order a bookshelf is arranged in. It starts with you because you are the constraint, then sets direction, then creates demand, then converts it, then builds the machine that delivers, and only then counts the money. Start with the question that is actually yours, which is usually the one you avoid.

  • 01The OwnerAm I the reason this business cannot grow? This block covers mindset, psychology, productivity, leadership and the way you decide.
  • 02DirectionWhere do we play, and why would anyone choose us? This block covers strategy, business models, revenue models and strategic choice.
  • 03DemandWhy do they not know we exist? This block covers marketing, branding, advertising and market awareness.
  • 04RevenueHow do we sell without it depending on me? This block covers selling and the mechanisms behind it.
  • 05The MachineWill this run without me in the room? This block covers people, culture, process and the way the work actually runs.
  • 06MoneyWhere is the cash going, and what should we charge? This block covers cash, pricing, and both company and personal finance.
