03 Paying

You pay before GST, and the page shows you the total.

  • 01PaymentPayments are collected through Razorpay. SB Coaching Services does not see or store your card number, your bank login or your UPI credentials at any point.
  • 02Prices are before GSTEvery price on this site is stated before GST, and GST is added at the rate in force on the day you pay. The 2 memberships are the exception, because the price you see for them already includes GST. Inside Tamil Nadu the tax splits into CGST and SGST at 9 percent each, and outside Tamil Nadu it is a single IGST line at 18 percent. The payment page shows you the full total before you pay.

  • 04The monthly membershipArangam and The Table are collected by a standing instruction inside your own UPI app. You approve it once, and after that the amount is taken on the same day each month until you stop it. You are told before each attempt as your bank requires.
  • 05A failed paymentIf a payment fails, you are told on WhatsApp and given 7 days to set it right. If it is not settled in that time, access pauses until it is.
  • 06The founding priceAnyone who joins on or before 2 October 2026 keeps the price he joined at until October 2029. The lock belongs to the membership rather than to the person, so a member who stops and comes back later pays the price on the day he returns.
  • 07Price changesA price may change. If it changes for a membership you already hold, and you are not inside the founding price lock, you are told at least 30 days before it applies and you may stop before it does.
