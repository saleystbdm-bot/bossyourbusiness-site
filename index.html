What happens in it

03 Who this is for

I work with businesses between ₹1 crore and ₹100 crore, and I say no more often than I say yes.

At ₹1 crore you are still inside every decision, and the thing holding the business down is almost always you. At ₹100 crore you have a team and the problem has moved into the structure. Both are my work. Neither is the same conversation. Here is the standard, in figures rather than in adjectives, so you can check it yourself before you spend 90 minutes on me.

I will take the work when

  • 01The business turns over ₹1 crore or more. Below that the problem is usually the offer rather than the structure, and coaching is the wrong instrument.
  • 02The business is 3 years old or more, so there is a pattern to read rather than a hope.
  • 03You will release your figures. I do not diagnose a business from a conversation.
  • 04You hold the authority to change how the business is built, not only how you think about it.
  • 05You can act on one thing in a week without waiting for a plan.

I will not take the work when

  • 01You have not started yet, or you are running a business alongside a job.
  • 02You want energy in the room. Motivation wears off by Monday morning and I will not sell it.
  • 03You want the prescription without the measurement. I do not guess at a business.
  • 04The numbers stay closed. A coach working from your summary is being paid to agree with you.
  • 05There is an unresolved dispute inside the family or the partnership. You have to settle that first, because no structure survives it.

This is not a filter I am proud of. It is the only honest way to work when the thing being bought is my own judgement. I also run a small number of one to one engagements at a time, and that is a fact about capacity rather than a reason to hurry.

05 Not sure which one

Answer 3 questions and I will tell you which of the 7 to take.

It takes about 20 seconds and nothing is collected. I would rather you took the right ₹2,000 thing than the wrong ₹50,000 one.

This is a rough sorting rather than a diagnosis. A real diagnosis needs your figures, and that is what the 90 minute call is for.

06 Take the measurement now

You read 10 statements in 2 minutes and you get a score you can act on this week.

This is the short form of the instrument I use in the room, and it is not gated. No name, no number, nothing collected. Answer honestly rather than hopefully, because a score you argue with is worth more than one you enjoy.

This is a reading rather than a diagnosis. A diagnosis needs your figures in front of both of us, and that is what the 90 minutes is for.

07 The method

My method is called the Business Machine.

In plain words, there are 3 things every business runs on. They are how it earns, how it delivers, and how it handles money.

Every business I have opened up runs on 3 engines, and they are the revenue engine, the operating engine and the financial engine. Under those 3 sit 9 working parts, and under those sit 27 things somebody actually does on a Tuesday. Nothing here is clever. It is simply a way of finding which of the 27 is holding the other 26 down.

The one thing worth knowing about this drawing is that I do not start with it. I start with your figures. The drawing is where the finding gets placed once there is one.

08 About me

The market made me a business coach.

I came to Chennai in 2004 with no plan and took whatever work there was. I freelanced for a while and then I worked alone. In 2008 I started a training company of my own. I had been an entrepreneur since the age of 19, and by then I had been on the wrong side of most of my own advice.

Then came 2017. A client put a chequebook on the table and asked me to stop training and start coaching his business. It was not a plan of mine, and it was an invitation I had not asked for.

I have coached ever since, and that comes to around 95 businesses in 9 years.
