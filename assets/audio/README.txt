The 6 free extracts go in this folder, 3 for each edition.

File names, exactly as the pages expect them:
  sample-1.mp3     sample-2.mp3     sample-3.mp3      Puthaga Paravai, Tamil
  rg-sample-1.mp3  rg-sample-2.mp3  rg-sample-3.mp3   Reader's Guide, English

Each one is a 10 minute extract taken out of a full audio, and the pages say so
in those words. They are not whole episodes, because a whole episode runs as
long as the book needs and the first 12 measured between 30 minutes and just
under 4 hours.

Encoding: mp3, mono, 64 kbps, 44.1 kHz. A 10 minute extract lands at about
4.8 MB at that setting, which is the right budget for a phone on mobile data.

The pages load nothing until the visitor presses play.
