02 Why this one exists

Most founders are operators trapped inside their own businesses.

An operator reacts and an architect designs. An operator solves the problem in front of him today, and an architect designs out the problem that would have arrived tomorrow. Most founders never make that shift, and the business slowly becomes a sophisticated job they cannot leave.

You can usually feel it before you can name it. There are decisions reaching you that you should not be making at all. There are problems that keep coming back wearing a slightly different shape. There is a team that waits to be told instead of running ahead. And there is a founder who is tired, indispensable and quietly stuck.

This session is for the founder who wants to think differently about the business so that he can stop running it and start designing it.
